How to read the journey

The selection highlights changes in interaction and appearance; it does not list every system. Historical scenes are original educational illustrations made with HTML and CSS. They are not system screenshots or reproductions of official photographs.

Apple II had built-in BASIC in 1977; Apple DOS arrived in 1978. Macintosh helped popularize the graphical interface. KDE and GNOME are desktop environments. Recent trends do not yet have the same historical perspective as earlier milestones.
