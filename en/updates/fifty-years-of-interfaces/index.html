DESIGN / October 25, 2026

Fifty years of new possibilities

How we selected the milestones in our interface journey, from 1977 to our design horizon.

Choosing moments that explain a change

This website selects milestones for their contribution to interaction and their visual recognizability. It begins with Apple II and text; continues through DOS, Xerox Star, Lisa and Macintosh; and explores Windows, KDE, GNOME and macOS.

The goal is to explain what changed in the relationship between a person and their computer. Hardware and versions help place those decisions in their time.

Illustrations and sources

The historical scenes in this first website are original educational illustrations built with HTML and CSS. They represent ideas from each era and are not exact system screenshots.

Each chapter links to sources from manufacturers, projects and the Computer History Museum. The sources page collects those references and credits for the material used.

A horizon being built today

The selection ends with VoyagerOS as a desktop proposal in development. The year 2027 expresses its design direction. Beta progress is described by its current status.

Understanding the background helps us ask better questions for the next chapter.
