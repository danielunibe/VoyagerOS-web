DEVELOPMENT / October 25, 2026

From an idea to a native beta

The project has a development beta. Public distribution needs its own preparation.

The native presentation

VoyagerOS’s active experience uses a native Windows host with D3D12, DirectComposition and a Rust core. The promotional website is a publication separate from that application.

Published captures show two specific snapshots: the native Dock on October 11, 2026, and Settings in the October 23, 2026 development beta. Website compositions add editorial backgrounds; captures retain their recorded appearance.

What “in beta” means here

A development version exists for reviewing part of the experience. Complete integration, visual validation, physical interaction and distribution each require their own checks.

The October 24 engineering review left a candidate prepared to continue integrated certification. That result alone does not represent a public release.

Before offering a download

The Beta page will publish the package and its version information when it is ready for distribution. It will include compatibility, requirements, signing status, notes and a checksum.

Until then, updates let you follow development while keeping a capture or a successful build distinct from acceptance of the complete experience.
