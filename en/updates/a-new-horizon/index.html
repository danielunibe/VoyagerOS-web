PROJECT / October 25, 2026

A new horizon for the desktop

VoyagerOS has a place to tell its story. Welcome to the beginning of the journey.

A project taking shape

VoyagerOS explores a desktop experience for Windows with a visual language of its own. Access to apps, space organization, controls and personalization are the areas we are developing.

This website exists to explain that work clearly: what we are exploring, what appears in the beta and what needs more testing.

A story that brings us here

The journey begins in 1977. Through commands, desktops, windows and new ways to interact, we revisit some of the decisions that made computers everyday tools.

VoyagerOS closes that journey as a proposal in progress. 2027 is a design horizon, with no release date announced.

How to take part

Follow the updates through the RSS feed, share the project and return to discover its next steps. We are preparing a Buy Me a Coffee profile for voluntary support from this website.

Contributions will support development and testing. They are not a preorder or a promise of exclusive access.
